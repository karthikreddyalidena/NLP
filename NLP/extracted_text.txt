For NOC, format is attached below.(FOR ADDITIONAL INVENTORS, PLEASE ADD ROWS)3.DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION: Farmers often take crop and input decisions (seed selection, fertilizer use, irrigation, pest control) based on experience or fragmented information. Unpredictable weather patterns, changing rainfall, pest outbreaks and soil degradation increase the risk of low yield and economic loss.The Crop Guidance System is a machine learning–powered decision support platform that analyses weather data (temperature, humidity, rainfall, wind), pest risk indicators, and soil properties (pH, moisture, organic matter, fertility) to recommend:Suitable crops for a given season, region and fieldRisk level of pests and diseasesPreventive and control measures (irrigation, fertilizer, pesticide scheduling)The system uses regression models to estimate yield and risk scores, and clustering algorithms to group locations / farms into similar agro-climatic zones. It then generates personalized, location-specific guidance that can be delivered via web / mobile app, or dashboard for extension officers.PROBLEM ADDRESSED BY THE INVENTION:  Unscientific Crop SelectionFarmers often choose crops without fully considering rainfall, temperature pattern, soil health and market risk. This causes crop failure or poor yield.Delayed Identification of Pest & Disease RiskPest / disease advisories are often generic and delayed. Farmers do not get early-warning based on local weather, crop stage, and historical risk patterns.Inefficient Use of Water and InputsOver-irrigation, overuse of fertilizers and pesticides increases cost and harms soil health. There is no data-driven scheduling based on upcoming rainfall and soil moisture.Fragmented Information SourcesWeather forecast, soil test reports, and government advisories are scattered. Farmers lack a single integrated platform that converts all this data into simple, actionable advice.The Crop Guidance System addresses these issues by continuously analysing multi-source data and transforming it into clear recommendations and precautions.OBJECTIVE OF THE INVENTION (Provide minimum two)AI-Based Crop RecommendationTo suggest the most suitable crops for a given region, season, and soil condition using machine learning models trained on historical yield, weather, and soil data.Early Pest and Disease Risk AssessmentTo estimate pest / disease risk based on weather patterns, crop stage, and historical outbreak data, and to generate early warnings with preventive measures.Smart Irrigation and Input GuidanceTo provide recommendations for irrigation scheduling, fertilizer application, and pesticide usage based on predicted rainfall, soil moisture and crop requirement.Farmer-Friendly Decision Support PlatformTo deliver complex ML outputs as simple, vernacular-language advisories via mobile / web interface that farmers and extension officers can easily understand and use.C.STATE OF THE ART/ RESEARCH GAP/NOVELTY:  Describe your invention fulfil the research gap?
